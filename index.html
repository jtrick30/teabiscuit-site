For adult children and family caregivers

Know what the doctor said, and get it done.

Your parent presses one button at the doctor's visit. Tea Biscuit turns the visit into a clear plan on your phone, with the reason for each step. Then it helps you finish that plan in one sitting.

Built by a family physician, for families without a doctor in the family.

The visit is the easy part

Afterwards there's a referral to chase, a new prescription, a test to book, and clinics that don't talk to each other. Most families don't have a doctor to ask.

What you get

  • Your parent presses one button at the visit. Nobody has to take notes.
  • You both get a plan with the reason for each step, in plain words.
  • One sitting to make every call, with the numbers, scripts and forms ready.
  • Tell us what matters to you. We find the services that fit.
  • Your parent stays in charge. You help as much as they want.
  • You can see where each step stands:
✓ Done◷ Waiting on clinic! Stuck

Why it's different

  • We get it done, not just tell you. Other apps stop at a summary. Tea Biscuit takes you through every call and form in one sitting, and shows you what's still waiting.
  • Your data and your choices stay yours. We never sell your data, and no one pays us to send you to a service. You choose every provider.
  • Built by a family physician who knows where plans fall through after the visit, and how to close those gaps.

How it works

  1. Set it up together with your parent, in about 15 minutes.
  2. Press record at the next doctor visit.
  3. Finish the plan in one sitting and see what's done.
Tea Biscuit is opening to its first families and organizations soon. It does not give medical advice and does not replace your doctor. The people and details shown are examples. Ottawa, Ontario. A project by Jason Trickovic, MD. Contact: jason@piezoelectric.ai
